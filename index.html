Ghost Cam

Shoot your plant any way you like. Tap the pot rim twice. See how much it grew since last time.

  1. Add a plant and shoot it, pot in frame.
  2. Tap the left and right edges of the pot rim.
  3. Next time, the app lines the two photos up by the pot and shows the change.

Your browser will ask for camera permission.

Photos stay in this device's browser.
Nothing is sent to a server, and no location is recorded. Privacy

Tip: Add to Home Screen so your photos are kept safely.
