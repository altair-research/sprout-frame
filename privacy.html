Ghost Cam — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-24 · Altair Research Lab

Short version: Ghost Cam does not collect, send, or share any of your data. Everything stays on your device.

What the app uses

  • Camera — to take plant photos. The live view is processed only on your device, to compare it with your last photo.
  • Motion sensors — to read how your phone is tilted, so you can hold it at the same angle as last time.
  • Vibration — a short buzz when the angle matches (Android only).

Where your photos go

Photos, plant names, and the points you tap on the pot are stored in your browser's storage on this device. They are never uploaded. There is no account, no server, no analytics, and no ads.

Photos are also saved to your device's Downloads folder when you tap Save (you can turn this off). They leave your device only if you choose to share or export them yourself.

Photos taken in the app contain no location data.

Deleting your data

Delete photos one by one in the Log, or clear this site's data in your browser settings (or uninstall the app). Because nothing is stored elsewhere, that removes everything. Use Export all first if you want to keep a copy.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change. Questions: altair.research.lab@gmail.com
